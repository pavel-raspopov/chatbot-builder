# Northwind Desk — customer FAQ

Northwind Desk is a small helpdesk product for SaaS teams. This file is the knowledge base for the DocuChat demo bot.

## Refunds

The refund window is **14 days** from the purchase date.

To request a refund, email billing@northwind-desk.example with your order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method within five business days.

After 14 days we do not offer refunds. We can still help you use the product.

## Hours

Support chat is open Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00–17:00 Eastern Time.

We do not offer weekend support on the Free plan.

## Inviting a teammate

Open **Settings → Team**, then click **Invite**. Enter their work email.

Free accounts may invite **one** teammate. Pro accounts may invite up to ten.

Invites expire after seven days. If the link expires, send a new invite.

## Password reset

On the login page, click **Forgot password**. We email a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes.

If you do not see the email, check spam. We never ask for your password in chat.

## What this FAQ does not cover

This document does not include salaries, investor details, or internal roadmap dates. The bot should not invent those answers.
